View-aware object removal framework (**SPDP**) using diffusion-based per-view sampling for consistent multi-view background reconstruction.

- Outperforms OR-NeRF and NeRFiller on PSNR and masked perceptual metrics
- Suppresses corrupted views under severe multi-view inconsistency
- Includes a large-scale synthetic benchmark with cameras, depth, masks, and object-free ground truth
