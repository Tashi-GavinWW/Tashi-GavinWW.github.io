Reconstruction-aware multi-view object removal that learns continuous confidence distributions over independently inpainted views.

- Integrates 3DGS-based reliability estimation and conditional diffusion
- Designs reliability cues for geometry, appearance, depth, visibility, and viewpoint diversity
- Builds the CoRe-MV benchmark and improves Masked FID / warping error vs. NeRF and 3DGS baselines
